About

The region is the Environment Agency river catchments of the Tweed and Till, Coquet, Rede and upper North Tyne. Records beyond it are shown faded. Scotland is not covered: its designations are held by Historic Environment Scotland and NatureScot, and Scots law on finds differs.

Using a metal detector on a scheduled monument without written consent is an offence under s.42 of the Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Areas Act 1979. This map summarises public data and is not legal advice.
